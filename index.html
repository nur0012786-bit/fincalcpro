About FinCalc Pro

Helping Americans make smarter financial decisions since 2024

FinCalc Pro is a free financial calculator platform built for everyday Americans. Every tool is built on real IRS data, standard financial formulas, and updated annually to reflect current tax rates and regulations.

Our mission

Financial calculators shouldn't be buried behind paywalls or drowned in ads. We built FinCalc Pro because everyone deserves access to accurate, fast, and honest financial estimates — for free.

Accuracy

Our calculators use the same formulas used by banks, accountants, and financial planners. Tax brackets are sourced directly from IRS publications and updated every year. Results are estimates — always consult a licensed financial advisor for personalized advice.

Privacy

We do not store any numbers you enter. All calculations run entirely in your browser. No account, no tracking of your inputs, no selling of data.

Contact

Questions or feedback? Email us at hello@fincalcpro.com
